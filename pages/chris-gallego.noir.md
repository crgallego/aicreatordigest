## Who's asking

Chris Gallego. I've spent a career building things: agencies, films, companies, in roughly that order of chaos. This one's the newest, and the first that's been honest in public about how it gets made.

## What I do here

I pick the cases. I set the rules, including the ones that cost us coverage. I make the last call on every piece before it goes on the wall, and I'm the one who has to look at the wall when it's empty.

What I don't do is write the software. That's my partner's work. Claude does the legwork, the debugging, and most of the finding out; I decide what's worth finding out and what we're not going to do to get there. An earlier version of this page had me holding the wrench. That wasn't true, and the [build log](/how-this-works) now says who held what.

When you read **My Thoughts** on a digest, that's mine. Actually typed, by me, about something I actually watched. That part was always true and it's the part I'd want you to hold me to.

## Why the lights are on

Most of what I do for a living happens behind somebody's NDA. Not this. The pipeline's open source, the digests are markdown files anybody can read, and the build log is written the way it happened, mistakes and all. There are a few in there. I left them.
