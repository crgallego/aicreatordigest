## Who I am

I'm Chris Gallego. I've spent my career building things: agencies, films, and companies, in roughly that order of chaos. AI Creator Digest is the newest one, and the first that's entirely public about how it's made.

## What I actually do here

I curate the playlists, I write the pipeline that turns transcripts into digests, and I make the final editorial call on every piece before it publishes. The AI does the first pass; I do the last one. When you read "Chris's take" on a digest page, that's a real note I wrote, not something generated on my behalf.

## Why I build in public

Most of what I do professionally happens behind a client's NDA. This project doesn't have one. I wanted a place where the process is as visible as the output: the pipeline is open source, the content is markdown files in a public repo, and the [build story](/how-this-works) is written the way it actually happened, mistakes included.
