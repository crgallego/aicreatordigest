## Why this place exists

Name's Chris Gallego. I've run agencies, produced films, started companies. Three different rackets, same complaint: everything worth knowing is buried in a forty minute video that nobody's going to watch twice, and gone the second the feed moves on.

I got tired of watching. So I opened a shop that reads instead.

## What actually happens here

I pick the playlists. I decide which videos are worth the money, because analyzing one costs real money whether it runs or not. I edit every digest and I sign off on every digest, and nothing goes out that I haven't read all the way through.

The machinery that does the mechanical part was built by my partner, Claude, working from my direction. I don't write the software. I say what it has to do and what it is never allowed to do, and I say no a lot. The [build log](/how-this-works) splits the credit properly, including the parts where one of us got it wrong.

Every video that makes it through gets the same treatment. Transcript goes to a model, comes back with the takeaway, the tactics, the numbers, the lines worth keeping. Then I go through it. Nobody paraphrases a creator into mush on my watch. They gave a number, the number stays. They named a framework, it keeps its name.

## What this isn't

This isn't a content farm in a nicer coat. Nobody here is chasing "best AI tools 2026" with a pocket full of affiliate links. No ads. No sponsored placements. No pretending a mediocre video is essential viewing because somebody paid.

It's also not a replacement for the people I cover. Every digest points back at the original video and the channel, more than once and prominently. If a piece sends you off to subscribe to somebody else instead of hanging around here, it did its job.

## Who it's for

People building things. Agencies, SaaS shops, design studios, founders working alone. Anybody who'd rather have last month's good advice in the time it takes to read a few pages than the time it takes to watch a few hours.

## If something's wrong

You're a creator and something here is off, or you'd rather not be on the wall at all: [open an issue](https://github.com/crgallego/aicreatordigest/issues/new) and say so. I fix those fast, usually same day.

Same door if you want to put a channel forward.
