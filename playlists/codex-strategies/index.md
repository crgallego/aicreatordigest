## Where They Agree

- Silicon Valley Girl reports Tibo’s claim that agents are finally reliable enough for non-technical knowledge workers—the same leverage early adopters spent two years earning—within months, not years.
- Knowledge work shifts on a short clock: the interview frames a meaningful change inside roughly six months, not a distant roadmap.
- Scheduled Codex workflows compress a marketer’s day—research, inbox, and calendar agents running without constant babysitting.
- Auto review is what unlocks longer autonomy; without it, agents stay short-leash.
- Half of Codex use is already non-code, per the conversation—knowledge work, not just engineering.
- Tone of voice still needs examples; local files give way to cloud memory as the practical setup.
- Vibe coding still needs technical skill to scale—Silicon Valley Girl’s framing keeps that limit explicit.
- Humans stay on the hook for outcomes, and prompting skill stops being the primary moat.
